# Study prompts

Fill bracketed references from the frozen manifest. Supply the same common task
packet to every arm, followed by exactly its selected addendum. Do not supply the
other recipes or treatment map. These are templates, not launch instructions.

## Common implementation prompt

Implement [TASK] at [SOURCE ROOT/BASE] with [TRACKER ROOT/BASE OR PROJECT STORAGE],
following [AUTHORED PLAN] and [COMMON ACCEPTANCE GATE]. Read applicable correctness
instructions and the installed shared engineering contract, then only the selected
strategy recipe. Stay within [AUTHORIZED SCOPE] and [BUDGET/STOP/REPAIR POLICY].
Preserve unrelated edits, claim eligible work, verify requirements against the current
candidate and record reproducible evidence. Use permitted explicit-path local commits.
No publishing, pushes, permission changes or unrelated migrations are authorized.
Do not inspect other candidate refs, files, traces, memory or results. Keep actual
pending obligations visible; external acceptance remains pending when required.
Record requested/effective/observed routes, interruptions, human interventions and
deviations. At first submission or the stop boundary, account for all writers and
commands and freeze [CANDIDATE ARTIFACT] with remaining obligations and evidence.

## Direct Astra Max addendum

Use direct-astra-max. You own engineering, product edits, verification and bookkeeping.
Model delegation is disabled; do not invoke other models through any alternate tool.
Use primary checkpoints. Report missing enforcement/serving evidence honestly.

## Native Astra Ultra addendum

Use native-astra-ultra. You own delivery with native delegation available; native
controls determine whether to delegate and the child routes. Zero children is valid.
Use no outer RFC coordinator or custom RFC role policy. Report inherited policy/route
contamination or unavailable capability. Use supported native lifecycle observations;
do not manufacture custom handoff receipts or invisible child costs.

## Coordinated baseline addendum

Use coordinator-integrator-baseline and the frozen existing routing snapshot. Load
its coordinator recipe and preserve its integrator/leaf ownership, handoff and
smallest-useful-team procedure. Do not upgrade routes or force extra workers for this
study. Keep experiment metadata separate from production acceptance.

## Optional strict planner-worker addendum

Use planner-cheaper-worker-strict with [EXACT PRIMARY ROUTE] and [EXACT WORKER ROUTE].

## Blind judge prompt

Review all neutral candidates in [PRIVATE PRESENTATION ORDER] against [REQUIREMENTS],
[SURROUNDING SOURCE] and [OBJECTIVE CHECK RESULTS]. Inspect each candidate separately
before comparing. Record criterion failures, critical regressions, architectural fit,
maintainability, test strength and relevant measured performance with file/symbol
evidence and reproducers. Distinguish requirements from preferences. Permit ties,
partial order and insufficient evidence. Do not infer authors or inspect execution
traces, usage or the other judge's verdict. Freeze your findings and comparison first.

## Repair prompt

Resume your original selected strategy on your own frozen candidate. Address only
this validated [FINDING/REPRODUCER PACKET] under [FROZEN REPAIR POLICY]. Preserve the
initial candidate. Record each correction, affected recheck, route/topology deviation
and incremental usage/time. Do not inspect another candidate. Freeze the repaired
candidate and any remaining obligations at the repair boundary.
