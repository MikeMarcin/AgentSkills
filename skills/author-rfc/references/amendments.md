# Revise a draft or propose an amendment

Determine whether the material is still an unadopted working draft or has authoritative
live requirements/plan provenance. Preserve supplied originals and prior decisions in
both cases. Resume from current artifacts and the pending frontier.

An unadopted draft may be revised directly. Record the change and its consequences;
preserve authored grouping unless the agreed change warrants regrouping. For a pack,
regenerate the view; reopen only affected confirmation. A source snapshot is evidence
of where the draft came from, not proof that its claims match the current code.

For adopted material, produce a **proposed amendment** beside the draft. Identify:

- Exact live RFC/card/plan sources and their revisions or content identities.
- Affected requirements, acceptance IDs, decisions and milestones.
- Rationale, before/after changes, preserved obligations and new/unresolved choices.
- Consequences for active work, dependencies and pending acceptance candidates.
- Proposed reconciliation and next authorized implementation action.

Keep live sources unchanged during authoring. Publication, active-writer reconciliation
and renewed bindings belong to authorized `implement-rfc` execution. Confirmation of
the proposal records shared design understanding; it does not publish the amendment.

When the amendment affects an adopted plan, read the companion's milestone-plan
amendment/adoption guidance. Existing adoption refuses conflicting intent or source provenance; replaying
adoption over a live plan is not an amendment route. Its observational
`check-amendment` preserves plan identity, scope, work selectors/obligations and exit
text. Use it for a compatible decomposition/refinement only when those invariants
hold. A changed requirement or exit requires explicit amendment/reconciliation;
neither renaming IDs nor a successful structural check bypasses that boundary.

Changed answers invalidate affected dependent branches and confirmation. Preserve
unaffected settled decisions, exact constraints and outstanding human/native/external
obligations. Record actual review coverage; a focused deep review cannot certify
the untouched proposal or its execution eligibility.
