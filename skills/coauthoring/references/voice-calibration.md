# Voice calibration

Use this checklist when a user asks to preserve their voice in a draft or match a set of writing samples.

Read the current draft and any samples the user has supplied or identified in the active workspace. Do not assume that a sample from another project or author applies. Prefer a nearby, recent sample in the same genre over a general style description.

Identify the author's choices before revising:

- Point of view, level of formality, and relationship to the reader.
- Typical sentence and paragraph length, including deliberate variation.
- How claims are supported with examples, measurements, or citations.
- Technical vocabulary, repeated terms, and intentional shorthand.
- How the author signals uncertainty, disagreement, humor, and conclusions.

Preserve the actual claim and stance. Edit for clarity and structure without flattening distinctive choices into generic prose. When samples conflict, follow the current draft or ask which sample should govern. Flag a claim that needs evidence instead of making it sound more certain.
