# Grill RFC

Combine `grill-me` interview mechanics with `author-rfc` artifacts. Resolve both
companions by skill name through the host's discovery and read the guidance for the
current operation; no fixed installation path or mandatory host Skill tool applies.
These two companions are required for a complete RFC grilling operation. Name any
unavailable capability, preserve the supplied draft/answers and leave that portion
pending. You may perform independent grounding or clearly scoped analysis with
available capabilities; do not silently substitute a complete interview/artifact
workflow. When a portable pack is requested, supplied or required at this stage,
`author-rfc` also resolves `implement-rfc` for its plan kit.

Carry the current scope, settled decisions, unanswered questions, artifact paths and
requested coverage into the companions. Resume from their records without repeating
intake. Determine whether this is new authoring, an unadopted working-draft revision or
a proposed amendment to adopted requirements. Author-rfc owns that boundary and
provenance; keep adopted live sources unchanged during this design review.

## Challenge the design

Ground claims in applicable instructions, implementation, existing RFCs and context
terminology. Challenge discrepancies between the supplied design and observed behavior.
Use concrete scenarios to expose material ambiguity, then interview with grill-me's
frontier/explicit-answer mechanics. Test these branches where relevant:

- Scope and behavior: observable result, ownership boundaries, exact constraints,
  numeric defaults, excluded scope and contradictory requirements.
- Interfaces and domain language: callers, inputs/outputs, overloaded terms, context
  boundaries and contracts between components.
- Failure cases: invalid inputs, interruption, retries, concurrency, partial effects,
  recovery and operational consequences.
- Compatibility and migration: existing users/data, changed defaults, rollout and
  rollback obligations, and adopted active work or pending acceptance.
- Acceptance: unique stable criteria that distinguish success from plausible failure,
  material decisions hidden by prose, and unavailable verification surfaces.
- Dependencies and milestones: prerequisite truth, usable integrated outcomes,
  preserved grouping/exits and all defined remaining work represented in canonical
  partial coverage. Structural validity alone does not settle material choices.

Cover material design decisions even when optional implementation detail is deferred.
Detailed internal design is available on request for the whole proposal or named
areas. A focused deep review explores that area and reports its actual coverage;
it does not certify untouched scope or force an interview of every internal choice.

## Keep the draft current

After every answered round, follow author-rfc's selected artifact shape. Update
affected RFC requirements/acceptance and inline decisions; a compact RFC stays compact.
For a portable pack, also update its decision record, affected cards/milestone JSON
and generated view. Changed decisions reopen dependent questions and affected
confirmation; preserve unaffected answers, exact constraints and authored grouping.
Record terminology/ADRs when material using author-rfc's criteria. Proposed project
adoption remains a separate later action.

Report material unanswered choices as unready for their affected scope. Distinguish
partial decomposition of defined requirements from unanswered design. An independently
ready current milestone can proceed under authorized implement-rfc even while future
scope remains unready. Finish with grill-me's concise summary and explicit confirmation,
recording scope/version and actual coverage. Confirmation is part of the design record;
grilling remains optional per task and creates no implement-rfc review gate.
